Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

EMS Cases ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by Hatsumi Takahashi. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the EMS Cases mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address: Used for account creation and recovery (reinstall / new device). Not shared with other users.
  • Username (display name): A name you choose that is shown on your posts to other users.
  • Profile information: Your professional role (paramedic, nurse, doctor, or other).
  • User content: Case reports and questions you post within the App.
  • Audio data: Voice recordings you submit for transcription. Audio is processed immediately and is not stored on our servers.
  • Purchase information: Transaction records for in-app purchases, processed by Apple. We do not store payment card details.
  • Usage data: General usage patterns to improve the App experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App and its features.
  • To manage your subscription and restore purchases.
  • To display case content — your username and professional role are shown; your name and email are never displayed.
  • To process voice recordings for transcription (via OpenAI Whisper). Recordings are not retained after transcription.
  • To generate AI-assisted responses using the Anthropic Claude API.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Anonymity

EMS Cases is designed with anonymity in mind. When you post a case or ask a question, only the username you choose and your professional role are visible to other users. Your name and email address are never displayed publicly. We recommend choosing a username that does not identify you.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – database and backend infrastructure
  • Anthropic Claude API – AI-assisted case analysis
  • OpenAI Whisper API – voice transcription
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management
  • Apple App Store – payment processing

5. Data Retention

You can log out in the App to remove the account information stored on your device, and you can delete your own posts at any time. To fully delete your account (including your registered email), contact us. Already-shared posts may remain after deletion due to the nature of the service.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

EMS Cases is intended for medical professionals aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 17.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
